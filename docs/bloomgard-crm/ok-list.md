# The OK list


**OK list** in the sidebar holds everything that waits on a person. The number beside it is how
many are open for you.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs your OK** | Changes that Bloomgard AI or an [automation](./automations.md) prepared: an email to a client, a status change, a log entry. |
| **To-dos** | Tasks someone gave you or the team, with a due date and the quote they are about. |
| **Done** | What was approved, dismissed or finished, and the result. |

## Approving

An approval shows exactly what will happen: the quote, the client, the change. For an email you
can edit the subject and the message before you approve. **Approve** runs it once, as you; the
same permission checks apply as if you had done it yourself. **Dismiss** changes nothing.

Nothing reaches a client without a person's OK. Emails and AI follow-ups from automations always
land here first, even when an automation is set to act on its own.

Unassigned approvals go to managers and admins. An agent sees approvals on their own quotes.

## To-dos

Use **New task** for a title, who it is for, a due date and an optional note. The person it is for
gets a notification. Tick it off when it is done. Overdue tasks show their date in red.

You can also ask Bloomgard AI: "remind Priya to send samples to Vardhman on Friday". It prepares
the task for your OK.
