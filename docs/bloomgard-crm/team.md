# Team, notes and notifications

## Team notes on a quote

Every quote has **Team notes** under its logs: internal comments that never go to the client.
Type `@` to mention someone; they get a notification that opens the quote. You can also ask
Bloomgard AI to add a note.

## Notifications

The bell beside your name in the sidebar shows what needs you: a mention, a task given to you, an
approval waiting, a message from an automation. Click one to go straight to it; **Mark all read**
clears the count.

## The Team page

**Team** has three tabs.

### People & activity

For managers and admins: each person's quotes, open pipeline value, approved quotes and value,
quotes quiet for more than a week and open tasks, and a feed of what moved (status changes, logged
updates, notes and finished tasks), newest first. Click a quote number to open it.

### Planning

A board the whole team plans on, like a Trello board. Work moves left to right through columns
(a new workspace starts with *Backlog, To do, In progress, Review, Done*).

- **Add a card** at the bottom of any column. Press **Enter** to add it.
- **Drag** a card to another column, or up and down to reorder.
- **Open a card** to assign it to someone, give it a due date and a priority, add labels, a
  description and a checklist, and link it to a quote by its number. The card shows the due date
  (amber when it is close, red when it is late), checklist progress, the linked quote and who has it.
- Assigning a card to someone tells them through the bell.
- **Mine**, **Overdue** and **High priority** narrow the board; the search finds cards by title,
  label, person or quote.
- Use the board picker to switch boards or start a new one ("Trade fair", "Exports Q1"). **Board**
  renames a board, adds, renames, reorders and removes columns, or deletes the board. Cards in a
  removed column move to the first column. Only a board's creator or a manager can change the
  board itself; anyone can work with its cards.

### Chat

Conversations inside the workspace, so quote talk stays next to the quotes.

- **#general** is for everyone. Make more channels with **+** (for example *#exports*).
- **Direct messages**: pick a teammate under *Direct messages* to talk one to one.
- Type `@` and a name to mention someone; they get a notification. A direct message always
  notifies the other person.
- A quote number like `QN-2026-012` in a message becomes a link that opens the quote.
- **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Hover a message of yours to edit or delete it.
- Unread counts show beside each conversation. New messages appear on their own while Chat is open.
