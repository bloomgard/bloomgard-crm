# Home: Bloomgard AI

**Home** is the first screen. It is built around Bloomgard AI, an assistant that can read your whole
workspace and, with your confirmation, change it.

## What is on the screen

- **Stats strip** (top): total quotes (with a trend line and pipeline value), quotes per status (click a status to ask about it), **My contribution**, and a **Pulse** of unread emails, clients waiting for a reply, and hot/warm/cold follow-ups.
- **Bloomgard AI** (the rest of the page, not a box): the conversation sits under your stats and latest quotes, with the command bar docked at the bottom. Type or dictate (microphone button), press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line, and **⌘K / Ctrl+K** from anywhere to jump to it.
- **Latest quotes** (the three newest) with a **Create quote** button beneath them.
- **Three suggestion chips** above the command bar, generated from your live data, for example "Summarize 3 unread emails". **Click the command bar** to drop up many more questions you can ask; type to filter them, and pick a template (marked *edit*) to fill it in yourself.

## What you can ask

| Do this | Example |
|---|---|
| Find and explain | "Find the Delta Logistics quote and summarise the conversation." |
| Query anything | "Which quotes over ₹50,000 have had no reply for 10 days?" |
| Read email | "Summarize my unread emails." |
| Charts | "Pie chart of quote value by client." |
| Create a quote | "Create a quote for Acme Steel, 40 of ITM-001 and 10 of ITM-014." |
| Send email | "Reply to Priya confirming delivery next week." |
| Change the catalogue | "Change the rate of ITM-001 to 95." |
| Review or change settings | "Explain my email setup." / "Set the AI tone to Friendly." |
| Update a quote | "Mark QN-2026-007 as Approved." |

## Changes always need your OK

The assistant reads freely, but **it never changes anything on its own**. When you ask for a change
it prepares an **action card** that shows exactly what will happen (the email text, the old and new
value, the new status). Nothing is applied until you press **Confirm**; **Dismiss** discards it.
Every confirmed action is re-checked on the server against your role and the allowed fields.

Agents can only see and change their own quotes. Catalogue edits and workspace AI settings are for
Managers and Admins.

## Transparency

Each answer shows the tools it used (for example *Searching quotes*, *Reading catalogue*) with how long
each took, and answers are grounded in your data. Charts and quote lists are clickable. Your conversation is
kept in your browser, and **New chat** starts fresh.
