# Studio


**Studio** is where Bloomgard AI shapes the workspace itself, the way an engineer on your team
would. Ask in plain words, for example:

- "Chase any quote that is quiet for 7 days with an AI follow-up for my OK"
- "Save a view of open quotes over ₹5,00,000, biggest first"
- "Make a Monday pipeline review skill"
- "Add a Visit log with outcome and next step to the quote form"
- "Check my workspace for problems and draft the fixes"

## Its tabs

**Build** (ask, and the drafts waiting to be applied) · **Automations** (every rule, made by you or
the AI, with on/off and its runs; Settings → Automations opens here) · **Views & skills** ·
**Health check** · **History** (what was applied, with Roll back).

## Drafts change nothing

What the AI builds is saved as a **draft change set**: a list of every change in plain words.
Nothing is live until someone presses **Apply** (managers and admins for anything the whole team
shares; anyone for their own views and skills). If one change cannot be made, none are.

Before proposing an automation the AI runs it as a **dry run** and tells you which quotes it would
touch today.

## Roll back

Every applied change set is kept under **History** with what it replaced. **Roll back** puts all of
it back in one step: the form, the automations, views, skills, AI settings and any data it fixed.

## What it can build

| | |
|---|---|
| **Automations** | Rules that act on quotes (see [Automations](./automations.md)). |
| **Quote views** | Saved filters that appear as tabs on Quotes. You can also save one from Quotes with **+ Save as view**. |
| **AI skills** | One-click instructions that appear as chips on Home. |
| **Form changes** | Modules and fields in the Field Configurator, including log modules. |
| **AI settings** | Tone, English level and standing instructions. |
| **Data fixes** | Corrections to specific quotes, each recorded in the Status Log. |

## Health check

**Check my workspace** looks for quotes whose form disagrees with their real status, statuses
outside your pipeline, open quotes with no client email or no value, a client spelled several
ways, quiet quotes nothing chases, overdue follow-ups, a subtotal typed by hand and a missing PDF
template. Findings with a ready fix can be drafted in one click.
