# Stats, charts and Bloomgard AI

## Statistics and charts

The old Dashboard page has been replaced by [Home](./ai-home.md). The numbers (total quotes, quotes per status,
your contribution, pipeline value) sit at the top of Home, and charts are drawn in the chat when you ask: "pie
chart of quote value by client", "line chart of quotes per month", and so on.

## Bloomgard AI (chat assistant)

**Bloomgard AI** now lives on the [Home](./ai-home.md) screen. It is a chat assistant that can read your pipeline in real
time, your inbox, and your Master Data setup, and can prepare changes for your confirmation. Try:

- "Most sold products"
- "Where are clients in follow-ups?"
- "Summarize inbox emails"
- "What master data is set up?"

It also supports **Deep Data Analysis** for longer analytical questions. Answers use only
your workspace's data, and usage is metered.
