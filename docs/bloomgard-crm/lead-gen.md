# Lead generation

**Lead Gen** holds enquiries that are not quotes yet. You do not have to find them: every client
email that is not a reply on an existing quote is read by Bloomgard AI as it arrives.

## What the AI reads from an enquiry

| | |
|---|---|
| **Is it an enquiry?** | Enquiry, reply, newsletter, spam or something else. Only enquiries become leads; the rest are kept as *Not leads* so the Inbox can label them, and you can overrule it with **It is an enquiry**. |
| **Who** | Name, company, designation, phone, GSTIN, city and website, from the signature and the body. |
| **What they want** | Each item in their own words, quantity and unit, and whether it is in your [Master Data](./master-data.md). A match by code or name brings the catalogue rate with it; an item that is not in the catalogue is kept and marked, never given an invented code or price. |
| **Returning client** | Whether they are already a client, with their last quote. |
| **A reply** | A short draft that thanks them and confirms what they asked for. It never states a price. |

## Hot, warm and cold

Each lead is scored 0–100 from plain signals, and the reasons are shown: writes from a company
domain, gives quantities, asks for items in your catalogue, says it is urgent or gives a date, is
a returning client, gave a phone number or GSTIN, is a repeat order. 65 and above is **hot**, 40
and above **warm**. A hot lead notifies managers; a lead that came to someone's own forwarding
address belongs to them and notifies them.

## Working a lead

Open one to see the contact card, what they asked for, the score and the drafted reply.

- **Send reply** sends the draft (edit it first if you like) and marks the lead *Contacted*.
- **Create quote** makes the quotation in one step: the client, line items from the matched
  catalogue, the next quote number, and the email linked to it. The lead shows the new quote.
- **Review in form** opens the quote form filled from the lead, to adjust before saving.
- Mark it **Qualified** or **Not a lead** (with a reason), give it to a teammate, add notes.

Tabs filter by status (New, Qualified, Contacted, Converted, Not leads), chips by temperature.

**Read the inbox for leads** reads older emails that came in before this existed, a batch at a
time. **Add a lead** takes an enquiry you paste in: a WhatsApp message, notes from a call.

You can also ask Bloomgard AI: "any hot leads today?", "turn the Shah Steel enquiry into a quote",
"give the Pune lead to Priya".
